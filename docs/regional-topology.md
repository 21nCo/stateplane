# STA-4 Railway regional topology and qualification

Status: definitions and a disposable qualification harness. No cell is operational until its connected provider and Cloudflare observations pass. The amended [STA-4 issue](https://linear.app/21n/issue/STA-4/03-provision-full-regional-cloudflare-topology-and-postgres) supersedes the former AWS RDS plan. Skillplane's control/cell split is retained; its historical region choices are not deployment requirements.

## Provider, regions and budget (2026-09-28)

[Railway's current region list](https://docs.railway.com/deployments/regions) maps `asia-southeast1-eqsg3a` to Singapore, `us-east4-eqdc4a` to Virginia and `europe-west4-drams3a` to Amsterdam. The provider region must be read back from **both** the deployed service instance and attached volume; a logical cell ID or DNS name is insufficient. Railway says moving a volume between regions entails migration and downtime. Recheck availability before provisioning.

| Environment | Control | Cells | PostgreSQL services |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Development | Virginia | `ap-southeast` Singapore, `us-east` Virginia, `eu-west` Amsterdam | 4 |
| Production | Virginia | `ap-southeast` Singapore, `us-east` Virginia | 3 |

Each control/cell is a separate Railway PostgreSQL service and volume with its own database, role, Hyperdrive config and protected credentials. Use separate Railway projects and Cloudflare accounts or equivalently isolated provider environments with distinct credentials; the private inventory records the project/environment IDs. Before promotion, compare both inventories and reject reused project, environment, service, volume or Hyperdrive IDs. The control database holds only identity and placement metadata; STA-5 owns the schema, routing and authorization. No production business content belongs in development.

Use a digest-approved Postgres image with pgvector available and Railway PITR support. [Railway's default Postgres template does not promise extensions](https://docs.railway.com/databases/postgresql); the image digest must be independently approved and `CREATE EXTENSION vector` plus a vector query must pass on a disposable copy before any environment is accepted. The private inventory records the expected image reference, approved immutable digest and database role for each resource; the verifier matches the image against the Railway service instance, then queries the expected database and role through its TCP proxy with the exact uploaded CA, hostname verification, PostgreSQL version and pgvector distance check. This local SQL proof still needs a real Cloudflare Worker transaction through Hyperdrive `verify-full`. A private-network or unverified TLS variant requires a separate proven path. Keep database URLs, passwords, deployment tokens and CA private material in provider/CI secrets, never in manifests, inventories, logs or PRs.

**Capacity assumption, pending measurement:** seven small services at 0.25 vCPU, 0.5 GiB RAM and 1 GiB volume each would cost about USD 71.05 per 730-hour month at [Railway's published resource rates](https://docs.railway.com/pricing) (7 × (0.25 × $20 + 0.5 × $10 + 1 × $0.15)). This is a planning envelope, not a reservation or quote. Plan subscription/minimums, actual CPU/RAM use, extra volume and snapshot blocks, PITR bucket storage, TCP proxy egress, Cloudflare requests/R2/Queues, logs and tax are outside it. Obtain a current project quote and spend cap before provisioning. One cache-disabled Hyperdrive pool per origin has `originConnectionLimit: 5`. The schema accepts only 5–20, the overlap between Cloudflare Free and Paid limits; it is a soft limit. Measure `max_connections`, active sessions under concurrent requests, reserve for migrations/PITR and p95 transaction timeouts. A load test must show acceptable headroom before increasing traffic.

[Railway volume backups](https://docs.railway.com/guides/postgres-backups-restores) are scheduled snapshots. Require a scheduled retention of at least one day in development and seven days in production, plus an unexpired backup with a provider snapshot ID and populated size readback. Railway's public `VolumeInstanceBackup` API has no status field, so an ID and timestamps alone cannot establish completion; the verifier also requires `externalId`, `usedMB`, `referencedMB` and `volumeInstanceSizeMB`. Treat this as snapshot readback, not proof that recovery works. Railway's daily schedule retains six days, so production needs an additional schedule with at least seven-day retention. [Railway PITR](https://docs.railway.com/volumes/point-in-time-recovery) uses WAL archives and creates a sibling service for recovery; enabling it requires a first base backup. The live verifier requests a recent restore estimate, but that is not a restore drill. The *planning alert target* is a latest restorable point within 30 minutes, with a recovery time target of 60 minutes. Neither is a promise until measured; record the provider's window, chosen restore timestamp, recovered rows, archive lag, restore duration and cutover duration. A successful logical dump restore is additional coverage, not a substitute for PITR.

## Placement and processing policy

| Data or work | Intended placement | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authority SQL, native full-text, pgvector, receipts, outbox | One Railway service/volume in the cell's physical region | Read service and volume region through connected provider API; no cross-cell SQL binding. |
| Identity/placement directory | Environment's Virginia control service | IDs and destination metadata only; no records, originals, search text or business logs. |
| Originals and exports | Private R2 bucket per cell, `apac`/`enam`/`weur` location hint | A hint is not a jurisdiction guarantee; verify actual bucket location. |
| Projection jobs | Cell Queue and DLQ, consumed by its job Worker | Delivery is at least once and Queue processing location is not guaranteed. The scaffold consumer fails closed. |
| Embedding calls | Disabled pending STA-12 | No text sent to an embedding provider by this ticket. |
| Worker execution and logs | Smart placement near the origin, default Cloudflare processing | Placement and telemetry do not guarantee geographic confinement. Avoid contentful global logs. |
| Backups/PITR | Railway volume snapshots and WAL archive for each service | Confirm region/retention and test recovery; verify the archive bucket's actual location and access separately. |

Reject strict country or jurisdiction policies until SQL, R2, Queue, Worker, embedding, telemetry, backups and support access have independently verified controls. The manifest rejects unknown strict-residency fields. `ap-southeast` identifies Singapore affinity, not India; `eu-west` identifies Amsterdam affinity, not Ireland. Historical `in-south` references in generated Worker names, service bindings, R2/Queue names, database names and disposable probe IDs must be migrated together. Old resources must be inventoried and retired after cutover; do not silently relabel a live database.

## Provisioning, qualification and rollback

The disposable qualification login receives `CONNECT` on its database and `USAGE, CREATE` on its disposable `public` schema so it can create the probe table. It must have no database or other user-schema `CREATE` grant, access to another user table, elevated role attributes, or membership in any other role. The Preview Worker checks these effective grants and fails closed before DDL. The protected SQL verifier checks operational control/cell roles for safe login, no other-role membership, database `CONNECT`, public schema `USAGE`, and no database or user-schema `CREATE`, including `public`; direct application table grants are expected after schema migration. The no-membership rule covers ordinary reader roles with `INHERIT FALSE` and `SET TRUE` as well as inherited and indirect grants. Both paths fail closed on missing or unavailable grants. Repeat checks after restore, redeployment and retries; a database owner or role with the expected name and an alternate reachable role does not qualify.

1. Confirm a clean exact commit, current Railway region list, project plan/quotas, pgvector-capable Postgres image/version, PITR feature availability and current budget. Run `pnpm topology:validate`, `pnpm check` and inspect `deployment/topology.json`. Provision separate development and production projects/environments and credentials; create one service and volume per declared control/cell in the exact region. Name services `<prefix>-<cell>-db` or `<prefix>-control-db`; configure `POSTGRES_DB` to the derived name (`stateplane_dev_ap_southeast`, etc.) and record its SQL readback. A read-only 2026-09-28 staging-service query confirmed Railway exposes these fields, without qualifying that service or its image for STA-4. Record an independently approved immutable image digest and the Railway deployment readback fields `source.image`, `latestDeployment.meta.image` and `latestDeployment.meta.imageDigest` for each control and cell. A tag alone does not pin image bytes; a missing or changed digest blocks promotion. Enable pgvector, scheduled volume backup and PITR; wait for a completed snapshot and base archive. Confirm production protections/access controls in Railway and a restore path before routes receive traffic.
2. Enable a Railway TCP proxy for each Postgres service. Record the provider-reported `domain` and `proxyPort`; that external port is not necessarily 5432. Require Postgres TLS with a verified certificate chain and hostname. Upload only the required CA to Cloudflare, create one Hyperdrive per database with `--caching-disabled`, `--sslmode verify-full`, `--ca-certificate-id <id>` and `--origin-connection-limit 5`. Configure its origin user as the inventory's `databaseRole`; read back the user, database, proxy, cache, CA and cap after creation, redeployment, restore and retry. A correct protected SQL URL does not prove the Worker uses that role. In each disposable Preview, query `current_user` through Hyperdrive and inspect effective grants before DDL; reject a stale, missing or elevated role. Keep passwords in protected input, never command history. If the Railway proxy certificate cannot pass `verify-full` against the proxy hostname, stop promotion and establish a verifiable TLS endpoint; `sslmode=require` is not acceptance. The API, MCP and job Workers of one cell share its single bounded Hyperdrive config. All authority, authentication, permissions/revocation, receipts and final search rechecks use the cache-disabled binding; writes do not invalidate Hyperdrive query cache.
3. Store a private mode-0600 inventory outside the repository or in ignored `.data/` with `environment`, `projectId`, `environmentId`, `control` and `cells` entries. Each entry has `serviceId`, `volumeInstanceId`, `hyperdriveId`, `postgresImage` (the Railway source image reference), `postgresImageDigest` (the independently approved `sha256:` digest), `databaseRole` and `network: "public-tls"`; optional `routes.app` and `routes.mcp` are distinct bare HTTPS authorities. Run `node scripts/topology.mjs compare <development-inventory.json> <production-inventory.json>` to reject reused provider scopes or bindings, including IDs differing only by hex case, and public route hosts shared by either app or MCP across environments. Create a separate mode-0600 JSON file mapping `control` and every cell ID to its protected PostgreSQL URL at the declared proxy, port, database and role, with no URL query options. Set `STATEPLANE_SQL_URLS_FILE=<absolute-path>` and `STATEPLANE_RAILWAY_ACCOUNT=<connected-alias>` when running `node scripts/verify-topology.mjs <environment> <inventory.json>`. This reads project-scoped Railway service, deployed region, image, volume, backup schedule, snapshot, PITR estimate and TCP proxy via Composio, Hyperdrive through Wrangler, and performs a CA-verified SQL identity/pgvector query. A missing or mismatched readback fails closed. It does not prove a successful Worker transaction or recovery. Render with `node scripts/topology.mjs render <environment> <inventory.json> <counterpart-inventory.json>`; the renderer rechecks both real inventories before writing any deployable config, including on retry. Dry-run each generated Wrangler config. `pnpm topology:dry-run` uses synthetic IDs and writes only under `.data/topology-dry-run/`.
4. Create distinct private R2 buckets with the manifest hints and separate Queue/DLQ pairs. Verify R2 actual location and access; do not enable public R2 domains. Deploy private directory/cell services first, job consumers only after durable projection handling exists, then gateways. The scaffold gateway fails closed until STA-5 routing and authorization. No production route is promoted from a dry-run.
5. For **each** declared cell, create an issue/head-named disposable Railway service/volume/database and least-privilege role in the matching physical region. Read back service, volume, database and role, apply migrations, then create a disposable Hyperdrive and Cloudflare Preview. Preview names encode the full 40-character clean checked-out head and cell: `s4-<head>-d-apse|use|euw` for development and `s4-<head>-p-apse|use` for production. `node scripts/qualification-config.mjs <name> <hyperdrive-id>` generates matched main and Preview bindings and declares the required `PROBE_TOKEN` secret. Supply a unique token through the protected process environment and run `node scripts/qualification-preview-secret.mjs <name>`; it rechecks the clean head and complete generated artifact before invoking project-local Wrangler through the current Node executable, creates the exact named Preview with a mode-0600 temporary secrets file, removes that file even on normal interruption, requires a returned HTTPS Preview URL, and reads back `PROBE_TOKEN` from the latest Preview deployment. An absent URL or binding blocks qualification. Never put the token in Wrangler `vars`, the manifest, a command argument or the acceptance record. Follow [Cloudflare's Preview secret configuration](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/previews/configuration/) if the command behavior changes. Confirm the real Worker sees the intended database/user **before** DDL. Run pgvector, transaction rollback/residual readback and fresh reads immediately after mutations. Then run `node scripts/qualification-load.mjs <name> 10 5` with the same protected token. The load command redeploys and reads back the exact-head named Preview, then derives the probe URL from the returned HTTPS Preview origin plus `/qualify`; it sends no token to a caller-selected destination or redirect target and forwards interruption to Preview setup. An optional fourth argument asserts an expected probe URL and fails before the first request if the new deployment returns a different URL. Record the name and URL in its output for subsequent evidence. It completes one serial probe to create the disposable table before issuing ten concurrent complete probes and requiring at least five observed spare Postgres connections. The budget counts all server client backends across roles and databases and subtracts superuser/role-reserved slots. It samples connections at probe completion; also measure provider peak sessions and Hyperdrive origin pooling during the run because the sample may miss a short peak. Record latency/session measurements per physical region, TLS and redaction checks. Then perform a timed Railway PITR restore to a sibling disposable service, compare data at the chosen point, and test the cutover/rollback path. Inspect the exact Preview with Aside Browser after provider and Cloudflare checks.
6. On failure, remove or disable public routes first, revert gateway service bindings to the last verified cells, and leave the source Railway volume untouched. A PITR fork is read-only evidence until its SQL identity, cell region, credentials, Hyperdrive origin and smoke tests pass; rotate secrets on cutover. Restore Queue consumers only after projections reconcile. Delete disposable Previews, Hyperdrives, CA uploads, TCP proxies and Railway services/volumes/projects, then read back absence through Wrangler and Composio. If any deletion fails, retain the exact ID in the private acceptance record and block completion.

The provider and Preview tests require connected accounts and real resources. Unit tests and Wrangler dry-runs only validate the static contract; they cannot close those acceptance boundaries.

## Verification risk matrix

| Changed surface | Failure to exclude | Focused check | Remaining live evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cell placement and inventory | Legacy Mumbai label, wrong physical region or reused dev/prod resource | Manifest and cross-environment inventory tests | Railway region, service and volume readback |
| Hyperdrive origin and secrets | Wrong image/database/role/proxy, reachable alternate role, caching, TLS CA or pool cap | Image and SQL identity checks, no-membership probe and operational tests, live-readback contract tests and secret-free render test | Real Worker TLS, pgvector and SQL identity |
| Mutation/retry boundary | Partial rollback, stale read or leftover probe row | Qualification Worker failure and cleanup tests | Per-cell Preview transaction and concurrent probe |
| Capacity and Preview secret | Other database/role sessions or reserved slots hide exhausted headroom; token absent from the Preview or sent to another host | Mixed-session budget, protected Preview setup and mismatched-target rejection tests | Provider peak sessions, Preview secret readback and concurrent headroom |
| Backup and interruption | Pending snapshot without provider size readback, unrestorable PITR or unsafe cutover | Backup/PITR readback tests and rollback runbook | Timed sibling restore, data comparison and cleanup |
| Worker/R2/Queue placement | False strict-jurisdiction claim | Unknown-policy rejection and 21 Wrangler dry-runs | R2 location, Queue/Worker processing review and Aside observation |
